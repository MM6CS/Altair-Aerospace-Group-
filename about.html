03 / FOUNDER-LED

A student founder.
A long flight plan.

ALTAIR is being developed by a third-year aerospace engineering student with the ambition to grow a technically credible aerospace venture.

The first chapter is individual research and focused projects. As the work develops, the aim is to collaborate with students, engineers, researchers and mentors—and to let demonstrable results lead the story.

04 / VENTURE ROADMAP

Earn the next milestone.

A practical founder checklist for growing from student projects into a credible venture. Keep the public progress honest; move each stage forward with evidence.

NOW / TECHNICAL PROOF

Ship one demonstrator

Choose a bounded aerospace problem, publish the method and document results, assumptions and limitations.

NEXT / USER DISCOVERY

Talk to real users

Understand who has the problem, how they handle it today and what would make a solution useful.

THEN / VALIDATION

Test a focused solution

Build the smallest safe, lawful prototype or software tool that can test the riskiest assumption.

AS EVIDENCE GROWS

Prepare the venture

Clarify the team, ownership, market, regulatory questions, funding needs and accelerator narrative.
