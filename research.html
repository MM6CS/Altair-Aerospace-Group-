01 / PROJECT LIFECYCLE

Small steps.
Traceable decisions.

Not every idea needs a prototype. Each project should have a question worth answering, a proportionate method and a clear statement of what the evidence supports.

01

Frame the mission

Describe the user or engineering need, boundary conditions, constraints and measurable success criteria.

REQUIREMENTS
02

Understand the physics

Use first-principles estimates and suitable analytical or numerical methods; make assumptions visible.

ANALYSIS
03

Compare and build

Evaluate alternatives, record trade-offs and prototype only where the learning justifies it.

DESIGN
04

Check the result

Verify against independent calculations, reference data or appropriately safe testing where feasible.

VERIFICATION
05

Publish the learning

Share the outcome, limitations, next steps and enough context for others to understand the work.

02 / PORTFOLIO STATUS LANGUAGE

Say exactly where
the work stands.

01

Proposed

An idea under consideration; no active work implied.

02

Concept

The scope is taking shape; assumptions and requirements are being defined.

03

In progress

Active work has started and the current milestone can be described.

04

Testing

A model, analysis or prototype is being checked against criteria.

05

Complete

The stated project deliverable is done—not necessarily flight-ready or certified.
